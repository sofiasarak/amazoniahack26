Rerouting Paths to Avoid Flooding, a Function - Submission Summary
Sofia Sarak

**Project Summary**

A Python function that reroutes to safer paths based on season and road surface.

**Project Materials**

Jupyter Notebook, all coding done in Python. Python environment specifications are included in requirements.txt. 
Particularly important libraries include networkx and scipy.spatial. 

Function should require relatively low processing power (other than what is required for very large data) and basic knowledge
of Python functions.

**Project Feasability**

Honestly – I don't know much about real-time routing. However, I hope that taking variables as simple as month and location 
(which can even be a pre-determined variable in a users system) when making routing decisions is not too technically complicated
to integrate in future renditions of Challenge 3 products.

**Potential for Impact**

In some areas of the Amazon, road flooding is a big issue. Incorporating seasonality and road crossings into routing can help
Amazon workers get from Point A to Point B more efficiently, and safer.

**Development Level during Hackathon**

In this hackathon, I only had the time and technical capability of incorporating one new data source, and therefore only routing
based on river crossing. It was also focused exclusively on Paragominas. 

The function developed most likely will not benefit Amazon workers on its own – it would have to be supplemented by accurate road
systems and a more robust routing system. However, I hope the logic can be of use for future development of this tool.

**Suggestions for the Future**

Information like month of year and location are currently manual inputs in the final function. In the future, I would hope
that the function is integrated into a system that can pull time and GPS coordinates directly from the user's device.

As mentioned within the function documentation, it may also be useful to incorporate recent rainfall information, if that is available and 
technically feasible. This can act as a replacement for the month variable, or contribute alongside it, when determining road 
safety in the context of flooding. Information about specific roads that flood often can also be useful to integrate.

Additionally, proximity to river (such as a road traveling alongside it), as opposed to just intersection, should be explored
for path weighting.
